Privacy Policy

Your personal information belongs to you. Here's how Personal Hub handles the information you store and the services used by the app.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Personal Hub ("the App") handles your information. Personal Hub is developed by the Personal Hub developer ("we", "us", or "our").

Data stored on your device

Personal Hub is designed to keep your personal data on your device. Information you enter — including finance records, health and vitals logs, vehicle records, documents, notes, and reminders — is stored locally in the app's database and is excluded from Android's automatic cloud backup.

Your personal records stay on your device.
We do not operate a server that stores this content, and we cannot see or access the personal records you enter into Personal Hub.

You can permanently delete this data at any time using the "Clear All Data" option in Settings. Note that files or photos you've separately saved to your device's storage, such as attached documents, are not deleted by this action.

Information collected automatically

Crash and diagnostic reports — Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive anonymous crash and error reports so we can identify and fix problems in the App. These reports may include information such as device model, operating system version, and the state of the App at the time of a crash.

Crash reports do not include the personal records you enter into the App. Crash reporting is enabled by default and can be turned off in Settings.

Advertising — Google AdMob

Personal Hub displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as advertising identifiers, device information, and ad interaction data to serve, personalize, and measure advertisements, subject to Google's policies and applicable laws.

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps and sites that use its services

Where required, such as for users in the EEA and UK, we request consent before showing personalized advertisements using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).

Users may change their advertising preferences through their device's Google Ads settings or through any privacy and consent option provided within the App.

What we don't do

  • We do not require account creation or login.
  • We do not sell your personal records.
  • We do not sync your finance, health, vehicle, document, notes, or reminder data to a server controlled by us.
  • We do not use your personal records to serve advertisements.

Children's privacy

Personal Hub is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data security

Your locally stored data benefits from your device's security measures, including the device lock screen and encryption at rest where supported by the operating system.

However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information stored on a device.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
